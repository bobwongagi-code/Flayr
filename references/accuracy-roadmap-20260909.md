# Accuracy work window: frozen 2026-09-09

## Objective and checkpoints

Improve evidence accuracy and relation/gap decisions while reducing human correction work. Technical completion is not semantic acceptance. This plan does not claim that current accuracy is production-ready.

- Week 4 checkpoint: 2026-10-07.
- Week 6 disposition: 2026-10-21.
- Only an already-started validation with explicit remaining work may finish beyond the window. A new hypothesis does not extend it. These are review dates, not scheduled background jobs.

## Immediate scope

1. Commit the bounded failed-stream diagnostic change after local verification. No timeout, retry, route, prompt or completion-acceptance changes.
2. Freeze one final supplement to the existing two-sample fact-correction probe: at most six calls, in the prior unfinished order (CREAMY revised r2, baseline r2; CARSLAN baseline r1, revised r1, revised r2, baseline r2). Preserve all earlier attempts.
3. Use the same saved payloads and resource limits, zero automatic retries/expansions/repair. Use the new diagnostic transport with source identity recorded; do not silently run the old archived transport. Fail preflight on an unverified compatibility change.
4. Stop on the first transport or contract failure. A further transport failure closes this probe as incomplete; do not schedule another supplement. Retain evidence for diagnosis/provider escalation. No new video extraction.
5. This small, development-only probe cannot establish production benefit. Record the time-separated original and supplemental calls; do not describe them as fully contemporaneous randomized controls.

## Business work

Keep two questions separate: whether Stage1 faithfully describes visible evidence, and whether Stage2 uses correct facts to select the right relation/gap. CREAMY and CARSLAN do not establish a single missing-product-value root cause. Do not introduce a category rule library, rewrite all S1-S6 rubrics, or add self-reflection calls without a separately approved experiment.

Production semantic repair still requires one evidenced mechanism covering at least 40% of the fixed error population, at least three distinct samples and two categories. Uncertain cases stay in the denominator. Similar labels and repeated calls are not independent samples or proof of one mechanism. Engineering faults with reproducible causes are handled separately.

Each admitted experiment freezes its single variable, concurrent baseline/candidate repetition budget, scoring opportunities and cost/latency limits before calls. Require the previously agreed improvement of at least 10 percentage points, protect existing correct results, and prohibit new two-band errors/false-large. Report coverage and unavailable predictions alongside conditional accuracy. More confident explanations or larger gaps are not gains. Failed candidates stop; another prompt revision needs a new decision.

## Human confirmation and validation

Use the existing simple Markdown confirmation flow: suggested S1-S6 conclusions and short reasons, then agreement or a correction. Preserve commercial observations separately ahead of stage detail. Do not ask the user to label ABCDE or fill machine schemas.

After 12 naturally arriving new samples, trigger the previously agreed one-time audit in queue order. Do not run extra samples just to reach the count. Lightly record correction volume and review time; use a small direct-human comparison before claiming drafts save time. Do not claim a draft-visible review is blind.

Passing candidates first undergo compatible historical regression, then validation on new samples unused in tuning. Preserve the existing semantic-acceptance protocol, including the 80% business target and separate coverage/severe-error requirements; this roadmap does not replace its denominators. Human workload must also improve. Resource-budget compliance alone does not prove an acceptable cost/benefit trade-off.

## End-of-window decision

- Qualified root cause and effective candidate: proceed to regression and fresh-sample acceptance.
- No qualified root cause or no passing candidate, but drafts demonstrably save time: reduce accuracy R&D priority, stop dedicated calls and repeated attribution work, and maintain model draft plus human confirmation as the current long-term delivery form.
- Drafts do not save time or mislead reviewers: narrow the product promise to evidence location/organization assistance; do not market automated relation/gap as reliable delivery.

Restart requires new independent evidence, an explicit validation plan and renewed budget approval. Rewording an old hypothesis, recalculating old samples or nearing a threshold is insufficient.

Root owns causal judgments, semantic rules, thresholds and admission decisions. The ordinary agent implements, executes approved calls and organizes evidence; uncertainty is escalated. No push or external provider support submission is authorized by this record.
